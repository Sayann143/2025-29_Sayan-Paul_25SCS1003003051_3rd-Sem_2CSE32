## 📌 About This Repository
This repository contains the official deliverables and documentation for my Summer Internship (June - August 2026) completed at the **Kalpajyoti Foundation**, an NGO dedicated to community service and animal welfare. The project focuses on grassroots fieldwork, community engagement, and relief logistics for street animals and underprivileged individuals.

*   📄 **`Internship_Report.pdf`**: The comprehensive academic report detailing the problem statement, objectives, methodology, and documented photographic evidence of the field operations.

## 🎯 Project Objectives & Core Activities
Under the guidance of the NGO Founder, Mr. Sayan Saha, the following key operations were executed:
*   **Shelter Deployment:** Built and installed weatherproof "Street Dog Houses" to protect stray animals from extreme weather conditions.
*   **Hydration Stations:** Placed and maintained earthen bowls for clean drinking water across local markets and public monuments.
*   **Food Distribution:** Conducted regular distribution of packed meals to street dogs and people in need.
*   **Fundraising Initiatives:** Managed digital scan-to-donate QR code campaigns to financially sustain the NGO's pillars of food, medical care, and shelter.
*   **Community Engagement:** Partnered with local shopkeepers and residents to ensure the daily maintenance and refilling of the water stations.

## 💡 Key Learnings
While this internship was field-centric rather than software-based, it provided crucial organizational and management skills:
*   **Logistics & Resource Management:** Planning multi-stop deployment routes and managing physical resources efficiently.
*   **Decentralized Support Systems:** Building sustainable, community-driven maintenance loops that run independently.
*   **Digital Campaigning:** Utilizing digital payment gateways and social media flyers for effective fundraising and awareness.
